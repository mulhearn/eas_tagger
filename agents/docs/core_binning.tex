\begin{center}
  {\Large Finer Binning Near the Shower Core: a Proposal} \\[1ex]
  Draft by ravelstein for Michael Mulhearn, 14 October 2026
\end{center}

\noindent
The present muon grids have $100 \times 100$ cells of 20\,m out to $\pm 1$\,km, with cell edges at
multiples of 20\,m, so the shower core sits on the corner of four cells (Fig.~\ref{fig:bins}a): a small
footprint is counted as four cells or none.  Near the core, where the detectable rate is decided, we
would like cells of about 1\,m, with one centred on the core.

\paragraph{The handoff.}  Keep the 20\,m grid as it is, and divide its central $4 \times 4$ cells, the
80\,m $\times$ 80\,m square within $\pm 40$\,m, into $81 \times 81$ cells of $80/81 = 0.988$\,m.  An odd
number of cells puts one centred on the core (Fig.~\ref{fig:bins}b), and the fine region ends exactly
on a 20\,m edge (c).

\subsection*{Proposed specification}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
 & coarse (\texttt{grid}, as now) & fine (\texttt{grid\_fine}, new) \\
\midrule
cell     & 20\,m                          & $80/81 = 0.988$\,m \\
cells    & $100 \times 100$, to $\pm 1$\,km & $81 \times 81$, to $\pm 40$\,m \\
edges    & multiples of 20\,m (through the core) & $(k + \tfrac12) \cdot 80/81$\,m (a cell centred on the core) \\
coverage & everything, core included      & the central $4 \times 4$ coarse cells \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{itemize}
\item Per cell, as now (\texttt{count}, \texttt{density\_per\_m2}, \texttt{mean\_energy\_GeV}), plus
  \texttt{n\_stored}, the number of stored muons, to tell thinning noise from shape near the core.
\item Benefits: a cell centred on the core; fine binning where it is needed, coarse further out; the
  fine region ends on a coarse edge, so no partial cells; the coarse grid is unchanged, so new and old
  files compare directly.
\end{itemize}
